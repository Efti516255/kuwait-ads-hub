# Kuwait Ads Hub

Create a landing page called "Kuwait Ads Hub" — a Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram) management agency for small businesses in Kuwait. Start with just the Navbar and Hero section — I will add more sections later.

BRAND & VISUAL STYLE: CLEAN, LIGHT, PROFESSIONAL SAAS STYLE

Background: light, clean — white or very soft off-white/light gray (#FFFFFF, #F7F7FB)

Primary accent color: gold/amber (#D4AF37 or #C9A227) used for buttons, highlights, icons — this is the brand color

Secondary accent: deep navy or charcoal (#1A1A2E or #16213E) for headline text and contrast elements

Cards: soft white cards with subtle shadow (not glass/blur), light rounded corners (12-16px), thin light gray border

Buttons: rounded-pill or rounded-rectangle, solid gold fill for primary, white with gold border for secondary

Typography: modern sans-serif (Inter or Poppins), bold large headlines in dark navy/charcoal, gray for body text

Overall feel: premium, trustworthy, corporate-grade — similar to modern SaaS product sites (think Stripe, Linear, Notion style layouts, but with a gold accent instead of blue/purple)

Small decorative elements: soft blurred color blobs in the background (very subtle, light gold/cream tones), floating icon badges with soft shadows

Fully mobile-first, fast-loading, smooth fade-in animation on load

SECTIONS TO BUILD NOW:

TECHNICAL:

Light, clean, professional look — NOT dark mode, NOT glassmorphism/blur cards

Fully responsive, mobile-first

Premium corporate feel, similar to top-tier SaaS landing pages
